## Aditivos y contaminantes

- [Safety and efficacy of feed additives consisting of tinctures from Rosa canina L.: Rosehip shell tincture for horses and rosehip tincture for all animal species (FEFANA asbl)](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10414)
  - Fuente: EFSA · Tipo: opinion_cientifica · Fecha: 2026-11-17 · Score: 19
  - Extracto oficial: Following a request from the European Commission, EFSA was asked to deliver a scientific opinion on the safety and efficacy of two tinctures obtained from the rosehip shells (rosehip shell tincture) or rosehip (rosehip tincture) of Rosa canina L. when used as sensory additives. The additives are water/ethanol tinctures, with a specified dry matter content of up to 11.0% (w/w) (for rosehip shell tincture) and in the range of 0.75%–1.80% (w/w) (for rosehip tincture). Rosehip shell tincture is proposed for use in feed for horses, while rosehip tincture is intended for use in feed or water for drinking for all animal species. The EFSA Panel on Additives and Products or Substances used in Animal Feed (FEEDAP) concluded that rosehip shell tincture is safe for horses at the maximum proposed use level of 2000 mg/kg complete feed. Rosehip tincture is safe for horses at the maximum proposed use level of 500 mg/kg complete feed and for all other animal species at 50 mg/kg complete feed. The FEEDAP Panel considered that the use of rosehip tincture in water for drinking alone or in combination with its use in feed is safe provided that the daily amount resulting from the exposure via feed alone at the maximum use level is not exceeded. No safety concern would arise for the consumer and the environment from the use of rosehip shell tincture or rosehip tincture in feed. The additives under assessment should be considered as irritant to skin and eyes and as dermal and respiratory sensitisers. Exposure of users by any route is considered a risk. Since rosehip preparations are recognised to flavour food and their function in feed would be essentially the same as that in food, no further demonstration of efficacy is considered necessary for rosehip shell tincture and rosehip tincture.
